RENDEZVOUS

There are a lot of people here, but you don’t see Agent Nightingale anywhere. She must not have arrived yet. The locked parcel sits before you like Pandora’s box in briefcase form. It wouldn’t hurt to just try to crack it open, would it? Nightingale may even be grateful you saved her the time.

But where to start? One double agent alone wouldn’t know the code, but together they have all the pieces.

Review the symbols you’ve gathered throughout your mission and cross-check them against your passport. Count your lucky stars that you’ve made it this far...but if you can crack this code and find out the Ringleader’s name, you’ll be the hero!
