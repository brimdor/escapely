THE SNAKE

The next double agent you must take down has been dubbed “The Snake” for an uncanny ability to slip out from under our noses, no matter how vigilant we are. Be on the lookout, Agent Bob. There is no telling where the Snake might have gone after leaving this coded message. The Snake might even still be here, watching you now.

This article is the first Snake transmission we have ever been able to intercept - and if you read closely it looks like it references four nearby clues. Can you find them? See what you can make of it, and remember - sometimes what’s left behind is just as important as the trail itself.
