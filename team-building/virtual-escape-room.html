ESCAPE THE BASEMENT

You respond to an ad for an open house, but when you arrive, the house looks abandoned – it’s falling apart. You’re intrigued, and your curiosity gets the best of you. You can’t help taking a look around. But as you go downstairs, you hear a door close behind you…and lock.

Fortunately, there’s an elevator to safety, but you need a code to escape. Find clues etched into the wall and underneath items – each one leads to another. Collaborate with your team to solve puzzles, crack codes, and open the elevator! Race against your coworkers – can your team escape first?

ESCAPE THE ICE PALACE

Aren’t the holidays magical? It’s a time for celebrating, giving gifts, holiday cheer, and spreading love and laughter. But this year, there has been some dark magic. An evil sorcerer has cast a hex over the land and covered it with dark snow and ice.

Fortunately, there’s a counterspell to reverse the hex, but it’s locked away in a dark, cold, ice palace. Luckily, the sorcerer left clues and puzzles that, if solved correctly, reveal the counterspell. Race against your coworkers – can your team break the hex first?

DESTINATION UNKNOWN

You are training to become a Level 2 Secret Agent with the International Secret Task Force (ISTF). On the last training day, you wake up with no memory of the night before. It’s your final test.

Follow a trail of clues worldwide and collaborate with your team to solve puzzles, crack codes, and find the secret headquarters! The first team to arrive will become Level 2 Secret Agents. Race against your coworkers – can your team arrive first?

VIRTUAL ESCAPE ROOM

ESCAPE THE DESERT ISLAND

You are out for a scenic aerial tour of a beautiful archipelago. It’s beautiful, and you’re having a marvelous time…when, out of nowhere, your pilot goes a little crazy and decides to leave you on the island.

Fortunately, there is an escape raft, but you need a code to start the engine. Follow a trail of clues around the island and collaborate with your team to solve puzzles, crack codes, and ultimately start the engine! Race against your coworkers – can your team escape first?

VIRTUAL ESCAPE ROOM

DOUBLE, DOUBLE, PUZZLE AND TROUBLE

A good-hearted witch spent years perfecting the perfect potion to save the world. But before she could put it to use, a dark wizard cast a spell on her so that she couldn’t remember the recipe. Fortunately, she left clues for just such a situation.

Collaborate with your team to solve puzzles, crack codes, and ultimately piece it back together! The first team to complete the potion will save the world. Race against your coworkers – can your team complete it first?

VIRTUAL ESCAPE ROOM

WHODUNNIT: CAN YOU CRACK THE CASE?

A mysterious murder. Six suspects. A string of clues. Investigate the evidence and solve puzzles to eliminate suspects, motives, and crime locations. In this interactive murder mystery escape room, you will work with your team to decipher clues, crack codes, and figure out whether each suspect has a reliable alibi. It is an escape-room-style game with a murder mystery theme – the best of both worlds!

Teams work together to solve the crime and at the end, we announce the winning team. Do you have what it takes to assemble the pieces and crack the case?

HOW OUR VIRTUAL ESCAPE ROOMS WORK

Escapely online escape rooms are designed to bring out teamwork, communication, and strategic thinking as teams complete challenges together.

Companies return to our virtual escape rooms because they're not just games. They're memorable experiences with fun virtual escape room puzzles and an exciting sense of competition. Your team will form lasting bonds - and of course leave with some funny stories to share.

  • Introduction to the Virtual Challenge: Start your adventure by logging in to meet your vibrant emcee! This energetic host will introduce the game, clarify the rules, and set the stage for an exciting virtual escape room experience.
  • Forming Dynamic Teams: Get ready to collaborate! Our technical team will seamlessly split participants into smaller groups, either randomly or as pre-arranged. Here, you'll meet your dedicated game master, who will steer your team through the virtual escape rooms.
  • Engaging in the Escape Quest: Dive into a world of puzzles and clues, working closely with your team to solve them swiftly. Your moderator is there to offer hints, ensuring you stay on track to escape – but remember, each hint might cost you! Can your team outpace the others and make a grand escape first?
  • Celebrating the Victors: Once all teams have completed their missions, it's time for the grand reveal. Reunite with everyone to discover which team emerged as the champions. But the fun doesn't end there – stick around for a bit of friendly banter and recap the exhilarating adventure!

Ready to embark on a thrilling virtual team-building adventure? Contact Escapely to book your event and create unforgettable team memories.

HOW WE CREATED OUR VIRTUAL ESCAPE ROOMS

Our founder, Rachel, is an escape room fanatic who has played hundreds of physical escape rooms. In early 2020, she created her first two virtual escape rooms to address the low morale so many people were experiencing because of the global pandemic. After many rounds of testing, those escape rooms were refined into the virtual escape room team-building events Escapely offers today.

  • Guaranteed Satisfaction: Every team experiences the triumph of escaping the virtual escape room.
  • Personalized Guidance: A dedicated moderator for each team enhances the experience.
  • Competitive Element: The fastest team with the fewest hints wins, adding a fun twist.
  • Customization Options: Tailor-made experiences to align with your team's unique culture.
  • Diverse Puzzles: Challenges require varied skills to utilize all team strengths.
  • High Staff-to-Participant Ratio: Ensuring attentive and high-quality service.

Escapely's virtual escape rooms aren't just games. They're tailor-made virtual adventure experiences that foster team spirit and collaboration, all from the comfort of your own space.
