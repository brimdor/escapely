      FAQ

      FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

      We don’t offer every type of team building activity in existence, but the ones we offer, we do really, really well. If you work with us, you will receive an expertly executed event that your employees will actually want to do again. We have friendly and engaging hosts, content that will challenge and unite your team, and almost 200,000 happy clients under our belt. We even offer completely custom events designed around your company, workplace, or anything else you can dream up!
      Our flagship team building game is our virtual escape room, which was designed specifically for virtual team building to engage and unite teams playing from anywhere. We soon after released an exciting virtual trivia format with live hosts that work one-on-one with each small team. Our virtual murder mystery is staffed with live actors who play the suspects your team must interrogate to solve a crime. We are always designing, testing and releasing new games!
      We offer in-person team building events that all have one thing in common: fun and friendly competition. Our Anywhere Adventure Quest is a combination of a scavenger hunt and an escape room that takes players to find locations, answer trivia, and solve puzzles to unlock their next stop. Our Urban Clue Quest is an immersive escape room adventure integrated with certain US cities (with more to come!). It takes players on an adventure around town to find real hidden clues, solve puzzles, and then go to the next destination to find their next clue! Our Art Heist is the perfect event for a team looking for an amazing team bonding experience all in one place. It's like a pop-up escape room that you can work anywhere. Teams get evidence and clue kits that they must race to solve the fastest. Finally, Amazing Escape Race is like a cross between an escape room and the Amazing Race, with challenge stations, live staff, and of course, clues and puzzles to solve.
      Many people will be working behind the scenes to make your event a success. On the event itself, the staffing depends on the event type, location, and budget. In our premium virtual team building activities, we provide one moderator for every small team so that everyone attending the event has personal attention. We also over virtual events that do not require staff. In our in-person team building experiences, we offer a range of activities from fully staffed anywhere in the country to remotely moderated to self-moderated. Your event coordinator can go over the differences and help you decide what is right for your event.
      All of our staffed virtual team builders come with technical support to manage breakout rooms, help everyone connect to their video conferencing platform, and troubleshoot anything else that might arise. Our in-person games sometimes work with a companion app, which is easy to use and requires no download!
      If you know which team building activity you want already, you can simply choose it and book. If you are not sure, our friendly event coordinators will ask a few questions about your group size, budget, and preferences and help guide you in the right direction. We have events to please groups of all kinds!
      The event organizer can be as involved as is preferred. Our event coordinators are skilled in executing our events and know how to quickly get all of the pertinent details as well as give you clear, easy action steps. They are always available for questions, and if your event is a large customization with many moving pieces, we will handle all of the heavy lifting for you!
      Team building is one of the best investments you can make in your team. When coworkers laugh together and make memories, it helps cement meaningful relationships and they actually look forward to coming to work! Give your team the gift of one of our amazing team building activities on your next retreat, company celebration, or to bring everyone closer together for that reason alone.
