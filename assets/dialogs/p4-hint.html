I CAN'T FIND THE REAL-WORLD CLUE

Make sure you’re at the Texas African American History Memorial. There is one view of this monument with plaques matching the arrangement on your coordinate cards.

HINT 1

First, locate your three coordinates. What information can you find on each one?

HINT 2

Take a look at the map. What information do you need about each coordinate to find the corresponding location on the map?

HINT 3

Can you put the information in the three map squares together to form two words?

HINT 4

What are those two words asking you to figure out?

ANSWER

NESKOWIN

EXPLANATION

Find the real-world clues - three plaques on the side of the Texas African American History Memorial. Each has a date range at the top of the plaque. They are:

Coordinate 1: 1528, 1820
Coordinate 2: 1836, 1860
Coordinate 3: 1877, 1954

Locate those coordinates on the map to isolate three squares. The letters inside are the ORE in Baltimore, the GON in Pentagon, and the CAPITAL in Capital City, which together spell OREGON CAPITAL. The answer to that is your solution.
