Hints

NEED A HAND?

There is no penalty for hints. Use them freely and frequently!

WHERE DO I START?

Review your screenshots of each agent’s icon and find them in the passport.

HINT 1

Did you screenshot the icons for each of the six agents as you exposed them? Where else have you seen those icons in the game?

HINT 2

Once you have identified a passport stamp to match each agent’s icon, note the shapes on the lockbox. Can you use those shapes and colors together with the passport stamps to get a number?

HINT 3

There are three colored stars on the lockbox. There are also stars in each passport stamp. How might you use those to get a three-digit code?

HINT 4

The stars on the lockbox are blue, green, and pink. Those are also the same three colors as the passport stamps. How might you figure out one number for each color?

HINT 5

Unlock code is 952

EXPLANATION

Each time you took down another agent, you were given an icon specific to that agent. Those icons are also found in some of the passport stamps. First, locate the six stamps with the matching icons.

Then, notice that there are three colored stars on the lockbox. Each passport stamp has a specific number of stars and is either blue, green, or pink.

Count the stars in those six passport stamps and combine those of the same color:

Book - Blue - 5 stars
Compass - Green - 1 star
Snake - Green - 2 stars
Bird - Blue - 4 stars
Radio Tower - Pink - 2 stars
Postage Stamp - Green - 2 stars

Enter the correct number of stars in the correct color order to open the lock. Then, open the lockbox to find the Ringleader’s ID card. Enter the name into the app!
