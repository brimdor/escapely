I CAN'T FIND THE REAL-WORLD CLUE

Look at the stars on the ground outside Paramount Theatre.

WHAT DO I NEED?

Look closely at the bolded code names in the article. Four of the stars match the code names in the article!

HINT 1

Look closely at the bolded names in the article. Do you see those names anywhere in the real world?

GIVE ME A HINT

Once you have identified the four stars you need, each has a surname. Search the grid on the Snake’s transmission for those surnames!

WHAT ARE THE SURNAMES?

MCCLINTON, FOSTER, WILLIAMS, SEARS

HINT 2

You should have found four names on the stars that match the four first names in the article. According to the article, you are looking for the corresponding surnames. Once you have the surnames, where else do you see them? Remember you are tracking down the Snake!

WHAT ARE THE SURNAMES?

MCCLINTON, FOSTER, WILLIAMS, SEARS

WHERE ELSE ARE THEY?

The grid of letters contains each surname - not necessarily in a straight line, but snaking around the grid. The letters spelling each surname can be found in order, touching one another.

NOW WHAT?

Cross off the four surnames “snaking” around the grid. Which letters are left?

HINT 3

The line, “Officials are advising citizens to avoid these individuals,” is a clue to what you must do with the surnames in the grid.

HINT 4

What is left in the grid after you “avoid” the four surnames?

EXPLANATION

There are stars outside the Paramount Theatre. Four of the stars match the code names in the article! Each star includes a different surname:
MCCLINTON
FOSTER
WILLIAMS
SEARS

Find those words in the grid (the letters in each word are connected but snake around the grid) and cross them out. The remaining letters spell your answer.
