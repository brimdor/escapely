There is no penalty for hints. Use them freely and frequently!

WHERE DO I START?

The name Scarlett Green references two colors - red and green. What do the red and green letters on the lanyard spell when you look at only those letters, in order?

WHAT IS IT?

1932 OLYMPICS WATER POLO MEDALISTS

HINT 1

The lanyard is quite colorful. Can you find something on the ID card to help decipher it?

WHAT IS IT?

The agent’s name: Scarlett Green.

GIVE ME A HINT

Use the article to determine the medalists: Hungary (gold), Germany (silver), and USA (bronze). Note that Hungary’s flag in 1932 did not look the same as it does today!

HINT 2

What do the scarlet (red) and green letters on the lanyard spell when you look at only those letters, in order?

WHAT IS IT?

1932 OLYMPICS WATER POLO MEDALISTS

NOW WHAT?

The three flags you need (Hungary, Germany, and the USA) are in two locations: on the box and on the back of the ID card. Align the flags on the ID card with the flags on the box and note where the arrow points.

HINT 3

Read the article to find the three medalists for each Olympic event!

WHO ARE THEY?

Hungary (gold), Germany (silver), USA (bronze)

HINT 4

Note that Hungary’s flag in 1932 did not look the same as it does today!

HINT 5

You have probably noticed those three flags in two locations: on the box and on the back of the ID card. Align the flags on the ID card over the flags on the game box and note where the arrow points.

EXPLANATION

First, use Scarlett Green’s name as a clue to solve the lanyard. Reading only the scarlet (red) and green letters, in order, spells 1932 OLYMPICS WATER POLO MEDALISTS. The article reveals that the medalists are Hungary (gold), Germany (silver), and the USA (bronze).

Locate the flags of those three countries on the back of the ID card. (Note that Hungary’s flag in 1932 is the version used in this game - it was different from the present-day flag.)

Align the flags from the ID card with the flags inside the game box to complete them. The red arrow on the ID card will then point at a piece of text:
Hungary points to BA
Germany points to CK
USA points to UP

Together, those letters spell your solution.
